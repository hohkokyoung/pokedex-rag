## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Calculators laid out for a phone
The catch rate SHALL put its inputs first: the status as one compact control, HP, and the situation (sliders and switches) in a folding panel. Then the best six balls SHALL show on a panel with "Show all", and the formula SHALL fold away. The type calculator SHALL group its results by verdict, each with a coloured multiplier: Takes more from, Resists, Immune to, and Hits. The damage calculator's set fields SHALL be settings rows, and its field options and math SHALL fold away.

#### Scenario: All balls
- **WHEN** the owner opens the catch rate for Dragonite
- **THEN** six balls show, and "Show all" reveals the rest, Dream Ball among them
