## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The website's answer layout
An answer SHALL open on its verdict (the first sentence, large, bold words in red) with no heading over it; later paragraphs SHALL be quieter, list rows SHALL lead with the Pokémon's artwork when a source names it, and citations SHALL be small red chips beside the text. A leading "Note:" SHALL be an amber notice. Starters and "Ask next" SHALL be rows in one panel, and "Ask next" SHALL follow the answer.

#### Scenario: A citation chip
- **WHEN** an answer cites [1]
- **THEN** the chip is a small box beside the text, not a bar across the line
